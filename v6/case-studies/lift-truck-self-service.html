Business challenge

Every issue a dealer or service vendor could not fix on site became a ticket for the manufacturer. The answers usually already existed across thousands of manuals, technical documents, knowledge base articles and past cases, but finding the right one quickly and confidently was hard.

As the product portfolio and documentation grew, service teams spent more time searching, escalating and waiting for manufacturer support. This led to:

  • Rising ticket volume: more escalations to the manufacturer and higher support costs.
  • Slow resolution: most tickets took 1–2 days to close, delaying dealers and service vendors.
  • Unused knowledge: institutional know-how spread across multiple repositories.
  • Stretched support teams: lower productivity under growing pressure from escalations.
  • Compliance risk: no easy way to confirm teams followed approved maintenance and repair guidance.

Evoke’s approach

Evoke partnered with the client to create a trusted self-service support system that reduces dependence on manufacturer support and enables faster fixes. Dealers and service partners ask questions in natural language and get answers grounded in approved company knowledge, each with source references they can check before acting.

  • Trusted answers: responses draw only on approved manuals, tickets and KB content; out-of-scope questions are declined.
  • Always current: new and revised manuals reach users automatically via Azure Event Grid and Azure Functions.
  • Secure access: Microsoft Entra ID RBAC limits the assistant to authorised users; demo and client environments never share a data plane.
  • Faster discovery: filters by product line, brand, region and content type, with one-click saved presets.
  • Proven at production scale: 2,413,646 search records migrated with zero mismatches; safeguards added after test peaks hit 208,534 tokens per minute.
