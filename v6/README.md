# Version 6 — "Signal"

Version 6 is a standalone design for AI Orbit: a monochrome instrument aesthetic with mint and ember signal accents, built around a WebGL neural network in the hero. It has dark and light themes.

It keeps all of Version 5's text, section order and heading hierarchy. Every page is generated by `tools/build_pages_v6.py` from the same content data that Version 5 uses. Every page also exists at the same path as in the other versions, so the version dropdown keeps you on the matching page.

## What it draws on

- **Version 3:** a cinematic, full-bleed WebGL hero with a graticule globe and limb ring behind the network.
- **decideai.xyz patterns** (studied, not copied):
  - the 12-column index grid with hairline columns;
  - crosshair marks and a dotted-line mesh;
  - a giant ticker with spinning stars and outlined words;
  - numbered "001." sections and thick-rule rows with an accent on hover;
  - line-fill animations.
- **Fonts:** PP Neue Montreal for display and body text; Jura (Google Fonts) for indices, labels and data.
- **No gradients** in text or CTAs. Emphasis uses solid colour.
- **CTAs:**
  - a black CTA with an animated outline: a solid segment runs around the border, and hovering draws the full outline;
  - a translucent glass CTA with blur.

## Layout (new for this version)

Header and hero:
- **Header:** an index-grid bar with numbered links and a theme toggle. It hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up.
- **Hero:** meta columns (a tag line, a cycling Build/Modernize/Govern index and the HQ coordinates), a giant headline, a side column with the lead text and CTAs, a proof strip, and the neural network behind everything.

Home sections, in order:
- **Ticker:** a giant marquee.
- **Platforms:** a row of hairline-divided cells.
- **Signal log:** the agent run, moved out of the hero. The prompt types in, steps reveal in sequence, the progress bar fills, and you can ask your own question with **/**.
- **About:** a statement, then three tall columns with big Jura numerals and line-fill tops.
- **Services:** numbered rows with a thick bottom rule. Hovering turns the rule mint.
- **How it works:** a diagonal staircase, with a step path that draws as you scroll.
- **Key facts:** a spec plate of oversized Jura figures and bars over a dotted mesh.
- **Case studies:** a sticky stack of panels that slide over one another.
- **Industries:** a line matrix with crosshair marks.
- **Knowledge hub:** an editorial layout with one featured item and a stacked list.
- **FAQ:** a Q.01–Q.04 accordion.
- **Horizon CTA:** a calm network with a slanted mesh behind.
- **Contact:** an underline-only form and world clocks.
- **Footer:** an outlined wordmark.

Service and case-study pages add:
- a spec sheet;
- the two approach paths;
- a capability list;
- an architecture chain with a travelling signal;
- the pipeline staircase;
- the case-study table of contents and article.

## Neural network — `assets/js/neural-gl.js`

- A 6-layer deep network built in three.js, with layers of 8, 14, 22, 22, 14 and 8 neurons, connected by sparse synapses.
- **Forward pulses:** mint pulses propagate forward synapse by synapse, activating neurons and lighting the synapses they pass.
- **Backward pulses:** ember pulses occasionally travel backward, like learning signals.
- **Pointer:**
  - the network turns to follow the pointer;
  - hovering excites nearby neurons;
  - clicking fires the nearest layer.
- **Themes:** additive glow on dark; ink on paper on light.
- **Safe zones:** up to four `[data-safe]` zones keep copy on a calm background.
- **Pausing:** it pauses off-screen, in a hidden tab, with reduced motion, or with the motion switch off (**M**, or the switch in the footer).

## Checks

- **Content parity:** all 1,350 of Version 5's text segments are present, and the h1–h3 heading lists are identical on all 6 pages.
- **Accessibility:** axe-core WCAG 2.1 AA reports 0 violations on all 6 pages in both themes.
- **Contrast against the live network** (worst case, pointer stirring the network): every hero text block is at least 5.81:1, in both themes, on desktop and phone.
- **Clipping:** no clipped or off-screen text and no horizontal overflow at 320–1440px.
