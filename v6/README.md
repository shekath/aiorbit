# Version 6 — "Spatial"

Version 6 is a completely re-imagined spatial layout for AI Orbit. It keeps all of Version 5's text, page structure and heading hierarchy. Every page is generated by `tools/build_pages_v6.py` from the same content data that Version 5 uses, which is imported from `tools/build_pages_v5.py`.

The site has six pages, at the same paths as every other version, so the version dropdown keeps you on the matching page:
- `index.html`
- `services/enterprise-agents.html`
- `services/ai-first-engineering.html`
- `services/ai-governance.html`
- `services/ai-advisory.html`
- `case-studies/lift-truck-self-service.html`

## Content parity (verified)

A Playwright check compares Version 5 and Version 6 page by page:
- **Text:** every visible text segment on a Version 5 page must also appear on the Version 6 page. Clock readouts are excluded.
- **Hierarchy:** the ordered list of `h1`–`h3` headings must be identical.

Result: 1,350 of 1,350 text segments are present, and the heading lists match on all six pages (45, 32, 32, 32, 32 and 10 headings).

Version 6 only adds interface text: keyboard hints, tab labels, telemetry and the motion switch. All of it is either `aria-hidden` or an accessible control.

## Visual system

- **Palette:** a void base (`#1E104E`) with electric coral (`#FF653F`) and cyber cobalt (`#2F2FE4`) energy. Surfaces are glass (`rgba(30, 16, 78, .45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`).
- **Type:** Space Grotesk for display, labels, keys and data; Plus Jakarta Sans for body text. There is no monospace and no Inter.
- **Tokens:** `:root` in `assets/css/v6.css`, mirrored one-to-one in `tokens.json` (W3C Design Tokens, importable with Tokens Studio for Figma).

## WebGL field — `assets/js/field-gl.js`

A three.js particle grid drawn in a single draw call: up to 150 × 88 points on desktop and 78 × 64 on phones.

- **Surface:** the grid rides slow procedural waves.
- **Cursor wake:** a 14-sample trail displaces the points with a velocity-weighted push and drags them along the cursor's momentum.
- **Click:** sends a pulse ring through the field.
- **Copy safe zone:** points fade out behind `[data-safe]` copy, with the feather placed outside the text box.
- **Measured contrast:** the worst case against the live, stirred-up field is at least 5.3:1 for the gradient headline and 9:1 or more for body copy.
- **Pausing:** the field pauses when it is off-screen, when the tab is hidden, under reduced motion, or when the motion switch is off.
