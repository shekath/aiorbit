[01] Approach

Two paths. One engine.

The same agent pipeline powers both — tuned for the problem you’re solving.

Guardrails & quality

Controls that run with the system, not after it.

Policy becomes code: inputs, outputs and actions are checked continuously, and every exception leaves evidence.

  • Input and output guardrails for PII, toxicity and scope
  • Quality gates graded against ground-truth sets
  • Human-in-the-loop approvals for high-risk actions
  • Immutable, append-only audit trails

We map your AI estate, classify risk and assemble the evidence your auditors and regulators will expect.

  • Audit packs aligned to your regulatory regime
